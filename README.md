# Hexapod Robot for Agricultural Applications
This project is designed for small-scale farming environments and aims to establish a research platform for a hexapod robot that integrates motion control, environmental perception and a fertiliser-application mechanism, with a view to progressively developing functions for terrain recognition, foot placement planning and autonomous fertiliser application.

## My Responsibilities
Served as project leader, responsible for the construction of a six-legged robotic platform, sensor integration, the development of environmental perception capabilities, and the design of a compact fertiliser-dispensing mechanism.

## Work Completed
- Completed the assembly of the mechanical structure and control hardware for the FNK0052 hexapod robot, enabling motion control of the physical robot and allowing independent operation to execute movement commands.
- Completed the hardware debugging and integration of the Pico Flexx depth camera and IMU.
- Established the transformation relationship between the camera, IMU, robot and world coordinate system, and utilised the IMU attitude matrix to compensate for point cloud orientation.
- Generated real-time 3D point clouds and 2D terrain elevation maps; the programme can process approximately 20,000 to 29,000 valid points per frame.
- Took full responsibility for the bespoke design of a small-scale fertiliser application mechanism; utilised SolidWorks for structural modelling and assembly design, and completed the assembly of the physical structure using 3D printing techniques.

## Future Plans
- Conduct experiments on dynamic mapping during robotic movement.
- Investigate foot placement planning based on terrain perception results.
- Complete the manufacture of a fertiliser application mechanism prototype, its integration onto the platform, and experiments on fertiliser application rate control.
